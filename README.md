# 🏨 Airbnb vs Hotel Pricing Analysis

Comparative analysis of Airbnb listings and traditional hotel bookings to understand pricing strategies, market positioning, and consumer preferences.

## 📊 Datasets

### Dataset 1: Hotel Booking Demand
- **Source:** [Kaggle - Hotel Booking Demand](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessemostipak/hotel-booking-demand)
- **Records:** ~119,000 hotel bookings
- **Features:** Hotel type, arrival date, stays, guests, ADR (price), cancellations, customer type
- **Time Period:** July 2015 - August 2017

### Dataset 2: Airbnb Open Data
- **Source:** [Kaggle - Airbnb Open Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arianazmoudeh/airbnbopendata)
- **Records:** Airbnb property listings
- **Features:** Price, location, room type, host info, reviews, ratings, availability

## 🎯 Research Objectives

1. Compare pricing strategies between Airbnb and traditional hotels
2. Analyze booking patterns and cancellation rates
3. Identify factors influencing pricing decisions
4. Test hypotheses about consumer preferences
5. Determine which platform offers better value

## 🔬 Research Hypotheses

**H1:** Airbnb has lower average nightly prices than hotels  
**H2:** Hotels have higher cancellation rates than Airbnb  
**H3:** Price correlates with customer ratings on both platforms  
**H4:** Length of stay differs significantly between platforms
